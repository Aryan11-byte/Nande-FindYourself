A Little About Me

I'm Aryan Nanda.
Let's make trade make sense.

My interest in international business grew through my exposure to the import-export industry and my father's experience in the field. I started this platform to make the concepts behind global trade easier to understand—one idea at a time.

From how buyers and suppliers connect to the details behind shipping, documentation, and commercial decisions, the aim is to explain the process clearly and practically.

"You don't need to know everything about global trade to begin learning. You just need to start asking the right questions."

Educational content, grounded in practical questions and a respect for the complexity of real-world business.
