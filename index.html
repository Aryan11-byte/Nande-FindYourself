The person behind the journal

I'm not starting with all the answers.
I'm starting with curiosity.

My name is Aryan Nanda. I'm 16, and I'm beginning to explore the business world through my father's 25 years of experience.

Watching and learning from someone who has spent decades in business has given me a valuable starting point. But I know that experience cannot simply be inherited. Understanding must be earned.

That's why I want to study how products find their markets, how businesses choose suppliers, how goods cross borders, and how trust turns conversations into lasting relationships.

Aryan's Theory is my way of documenting that process. It is a journal of lessons, questions, observations, and ideas as I learn about trade and business.

My father's experience gives me a foundation. My responsibility is to listen carefully, think independently, and build knowledge through effort.

— Aryan Nanda

The philosophy

Experience is the foundation.
Learning is the journey.

I believe a meaningful business journey begins with good questions, honest learning, and respect for the people who make it possible.

01

Stay curious.

Ask why things work the way they do. Research before assuming. Treat every conversation as an opportunity to understand something new.

02

Build on experience.

Learn from people who have been there before. Respect their lessons while developing the judgment to think for yourself.

03

Think long term.

Value trust over shortcuts, preparation over guesswork, and consistent progress over the pressure to know everything immediately.
